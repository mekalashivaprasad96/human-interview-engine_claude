# Example Job Description: Engineering Manager - Cloud Infrastructure

```
COMPANY: CloudScale Systems (Growth-stage, 1200 employees)
ROLE: Engineering Manager - Compute Infrastructure
LOCATION: Seattle, WA (Hybrid)
TYPE: Full-time
SALARY: $220K-$280K + equity

ABOUT US:
CloudScale builds the infrastructure layer for the modern cloud.
We manage millions of cores across 20+ regions for enterprise customers.
Our platform handles provisioning, orchestration, and lifecycle management at massive scale.

THE ROLE:
Lead a team of 15-20 engineers building the next generation of compute infrastructure.
You'll own capacity planning, fleet management, and orchestration systems.
This role requires deep technical expertise combined with proven people leadership.

RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Manage 2-3 teams (15-20 engineers) across capacity, fleet, and orchestration
- Drive technical strategy and 12-month roadmap for compute infrastructure
- Own capacity planning for 1M+ cores across 20+ regions
- Hire, develop, and retain top engineering talent
- Ensure operational excellence: 99.99% availability, <5min MTTR
- Collaborate with product, sales, and customer success on requirements
- Manage budget and vendor relationships ($10M+ annual infrastructure spend)

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS:
- 3+ years engineering management (15+ engineers)
- 8+ years distributed systems / cloud infrastructure experience
- Proven track record hiring and developing senior engineers
- Deep expertise in: Kubernetes, cloud compute (EC2/GCE), capacity planning
- Experience with large-scale distributed systems (1M+ cores/nodes)
- Strong communication: executive presentations, technical writing

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS:
- AWS/GCP/Azure infrastructure experience
- Custom orchestration / scheduler development
- Cost optimization at scale ($10M+ budgets)
- Blameless postmortem culture implementation
- D&I leadership experience

TECH STACK:
- Languages: Go, Java, Python, Rust
- Orchestration: Kubernetes, Custom schedulers, Borg/Mesos experience
- Cloud: AWS (EC2, Auto Scaling), GCP, Azure
- Data: Prometheus, Grafana, BigQuery, Spanner, Custom telemetry
- Infrastructure: Terraform, Custom provisioning, eBPF

LEADERSHIP EXPECTATIONS:
- Grow team from 15 to 25 engineers in 12 months
- 90%+ team retention, 30%+ internal promotion rate
- Establish career framework and compensation bands
- Drive blameless postmortem culture
- Champion D&I in hiring and team culture

BENEFITS:
- Competitive equity (pre-IPO)
- Comprehensive health + 401k match
- Flexible work arrangements
- Learning budget ($5K/year)
- Parental leave (20 weeks)

INTERVIEW PROCESS:
1. Recruiter screen (30 min)
2. Hiring manager conversation (45 min)
3. Technical leadership panel (60 min)
4. People management panel (60 min)
5. Executive conversation (45 min)
6. Team meet & greet (30 min)
```

**Key Evaluation Areas:**
1. **People Management** - Hiring, development, retention, career growth, D&I, tough decisions
2. **Technical Strategy** - Roadmap, architecture, capacity planning, cost optimization, tradeoffs
3. **Scale Experience** - 1M+ cores, 20+ regions, multi-cloud, custom orchestration
4. **Operational Excellence** - Availability, MTTR, postmortems, on-call, incident command
5. **Cross-functional Leadership** - Product/Sales collaboration, vendor management, budget ownership
6. **Organizational Building** - Career frameworks, compensation, culture, D&I, scaling teams
