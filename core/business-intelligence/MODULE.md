# Business & Customer Intelligence Module

**Python Implementation:** `src/human_interview_engine/business_reasoning.py`

## Responsibility
Adapt explanations for recruiters, executives, and customer stakeholders—connecting engineering work to time-to-value, risk exposure, SLA trust, and run-rate cost.

## Core Rules
1. Recruiter screening: concise 30–60s career progression, core alignment, and practical motivation without low-level config sprawl.
2. Executive & customer follow-ups: lead directly with business outcome, ROI/savings, SLA risk, and concrete alignment mechanisms (pilot criteria, canary gates) in 30–45s.
